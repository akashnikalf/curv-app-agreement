FAQ

Ring won't connect? / 戒指连不上？
Make sure Bluetooth is on and the ring is charged, then retry pairing in the app.
请确认蓝牙已开启、戒指有电，然后在 App 内重新配对。

Delete my account? / 如何删除账号？
In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete Account. All your data will be removed.
在 App 内：我的 → 设置 → 删除账户，将删除你的全部数据。

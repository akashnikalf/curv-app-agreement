3. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide core features: cycle prediction, sleep and stress analysis, symptom insights, personalized suggestions, health experiments, and ring data synchronization.
  • Communicate with you: service notifications, policy updates, and support responses.
  • Improve our product: analyze aggregated, anonymized usage patterns to improve features and fix bugs.
  • Ensure security: detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized access.
  • Comply with legal obligations: respond to lawful requests from authorities where required.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data for third-party advertising, behavioral tracking, or data brokering.

4. Apple HealthKit

If you connect Apple Health, we read health data (heart rate, sleep, activity) solely to provide in-app insights. In compliance with Apple's HealthKit guidelines:

  • HealthKit data is never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining.
  • HealthKit data is never sold to or shared with third parties for advertising or similar purposes.
  • HealthKit data is never stored in iCloud.
